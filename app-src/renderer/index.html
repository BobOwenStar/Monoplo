Legal & Privacy

What this app does with your data, in plain terms.

Privacy policy

This app runs entirely on your computer. Your settings — the Discord Application ID, your Custom Activity text, and the rest of this configuration — are saved to a local file in your user data folder and are never uploaded anywhere.

This app makes exactly two kinds of outbound connections:

  • To your own Discord desktop client, over your computer's local IPC connection, to set your Rich Presence. This never leaves your machine.
  • To Deezer's public search API, to look up cover art for whatever's currently playing. Only the song title and artist name are sent — as plain search text, with no account, login, or device identifier attached. No response is stored beyond a short in-memory cache used to avoid repeat lookups.

This app does not run in a browser, so it does not set or read cookies, and it does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services of any kind.

Terms of use

This app is provided as-is, for personal use, with no warranty of any kind. You're responsible for the Discord Application ID you connect and for anything your Custom Activity is set up to display — this app only sends what you've configured it to send.

Cover art is served by Deezer and remains the property of its respective rights holders; this app only links to it, it doesn't host or redistribute any of it.

Using a game or app's assets in your presence images requires the appropriate rights or permissions from that game or app; this app doesn't grant any.
